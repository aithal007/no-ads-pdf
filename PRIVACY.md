# Privacy Policy — Pocket PDF

**Short version: Pocket PDF collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing outside your own device.**

## What it does with your files

Pocket PDF opens, edits and converts files entirely on your device (phone or laptop), using your device's own
processing power. A file only leaves the tool you're using — Downloads on Android, or a normal browser download
on Chrome/Edge — when you choose **Save** or **Share**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, because the app has no
network access to do that with:

- The **Android app** declares no internet permission in its manifest. Android itself blocks it from making any
  network connection, regardless of what the code tries to do.
- The **Chrome/Edge extension** declares no permissions in its manifest, and requests none at install or while
  running. It cannot read your browsing activity, other tabs, or any website.

## What it collects

Nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no account, no advertising identifiers. There are no
servers behind this app for data to go to.

## Third-party services

None. The app doesn't call any API, ad network, or analytics service, on your device or anyone else's.

## Source code

Pocket PDF is open source. You can read exactly what it does, end to end, at
